this is not independent sky confirmation or a complete exclusion of smaller signals.

The nearest neighbor has no QLP Sector 17 or 25 product; explicit errors are
[retained](../../neighbor-322396629-20261010/results.json).

Spatial offset and the shallow SPOC measurement favor contamination near this
known binary. The exact contaminating component, period and class remain
unresolved; the catalog discrepancy must be resolved before identifying the
specific system. No new planet or binary discovery claim.
