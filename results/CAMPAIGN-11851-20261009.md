# Search of the full selected small-star population

All **11,851 selected stars now have a usable discovery-sector search**: 11,847
with TESS-SPOC and four with QLP only. This is the complete preselected population,
not a search of every TESS star, sector, transit period or planet size. No likely
new planet is established. TIC 80387959 is the strongest unresolved transit lead.

The executable `audit-complete-selection-20261009.py` verifies the selection set,
every saved target record, earlier run hashes, and non-overlap of interrupted
batches with their missing-target resumes. `checkpoint-11851-20261009.json` records
those hashes. Six original product failures and 143 transient batch-six errors
remain preserved alongside successful recovery. Original batches nine/ten were
terminated with exit 143; their historical manifests remain unchanged, with
interruption records and disjoint resumes. Every later batch completed without
additional technical errors. The separate random pilot is not added to this count.

## Strongest unresolved signal: TIC 80387959

A 4.192-day, approximately 800-ppm discovery signal appears in both SPOC and QLP.
An initial Sector 63 check showed fixed-time support but failed the preset timing
precision criterion. That result is retained. After explicitly reassigning the
already-inspected 63 to training with 10/36/37, a new ephemeris was frozen before
opening QLP 90/100. Both passed the original recovery rule: S/N 6.33 with four
supported events, and S/N 5.73 with two; depths 621/651 ppm. Published errors were
usable in both. Timing spreads 0.02477/0.02679 day are below half the frozen duration.

Discovery images are central; Sector 63 is offset. New TESScut 90/100 images and
empirical target/neighbor injections still do not uniquely identify the source.
Sector 90 has changing background; both raw and separately background-subtracted
versions are retained. The faint close neighbor hypothesis is not consistently
favored across sectors, but unresolved contamination remains possible. Counts
from dependent shifted-phase controls are not source probabilities or survey FAP.

Circular limb-darkened fits on already-opened SPOC sectors imply 2.52–2.59 Earth
radii **only under on-target, undiluted assumptions**. This range is model
sensitivity, not a confidence interval. Gaia variability-table nonmatches,
SIMBAD identification and zero nearby NASA TOI/confirmed rows do not establish
novelty. Initial NASA cone-query403 failures were retained before successful
rectangular queries. ASAS-SN's public SDK metadata endpoint refused connection;
no ground-based photometric conclusion follows. See the batch-eleven assessment
and followup-80387959-qlp90-100-20261009 evidence.

## Rejected or weakened signals

TIC 399450592's faint neighbor 399450579 shows ground-based eclipses at the frozen
TESS period and phase in three ZTF bands. Its position and plausible diluted depth
strongly support contamination, consistent with the discovery-sector pixels.
TIC 275633324 also has displaced images and eclipses near the discovery phase in
neighbor 275633326's ZTF photometry, with long-baseline phase smearing unresolved.

TIC 235596068 and companion priority 148894196 have deep secondary eclipses away
from half phase, favoring stellar systems. TIC 286395547 has deep eclipses and
strong orbital variability. Other weak or off-target signals remain documented;
automated pass flags are not physical classifications. Exposure-model companions
cover all completed batches, including original interrupted portions and resumes.
Final extra companion TIC 289239139 has broad structured variability and weak QLP support; it is unpromoted.

## Scope and limits

Selection emphasized nearby small stars with usable multi-sector coverage after
known-catalog filtering. Searches considered 0.5–20-day periods and three principal
peaks with alternative flattening and event diagnostics. The overlapping newer
QLP experiment searched 173 stars; it does not add unique targets. Earlier injection,
residual and positive-control results remain in the recovery/exposure-model audits.
Recovery is incomplete and heuristic; this is not a measured survey completeness
or calibrated false-positive probability. Independent extraction uses the same
photons. Original source snapshots, failures, download URLs and hashes are retained.

The active goal continues with source attribution and remaining validation of
TIC 80387959, investigation of viable sector coverage
outside the initial discovery choices. Repository remains private; no external
candidate submission or outreach has occurred. Test suite: 32 passing, including
injected localization and background-subtraction recovery.
