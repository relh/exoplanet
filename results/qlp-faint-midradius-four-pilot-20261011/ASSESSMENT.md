# Midradius four-sector pilot: 16 frozen targets

Eleven searches are usable: five below threshold and six flagged. Five other targets exceed the unchanged 120-day dense-search baseline limit, each spanning approximately 160.63 days. Original failures are retained, not counted as nondetections. Time-coverage-only recovery plans select sectors 97–98 for all five, retaining their original reserved sectors; the separate recovery searches are now complete (see the recovery assessment). They yield four below-threshold results and one variability-flagged result, with no eligible follow-ups. All 16 distinct pilot stars now have a usable window: nine below threshold and seven flagged. The five failed original four-sector attempts remain failures.

TIC 438792601 is the only peak admitted for follow-up. Native refinement gives 0.781550277 days and depth about 780 ppm, but timing predictions in reserved sectors 11 and 38 are inconclusive. The inspected raw Sector 99 and 100 difference images show a compact source about 2.21 and 2.03 pixels from the target, near faint TIC 288891067 and 438792607 among several blended catalog sources. This favors contamination without identifying the exact variable. The requested SPOC pixel product returned HTTP 404 and the failure is retained. No credible new planet candidate is claimed.

Controls were planned for the first three metadata-ranked stars. TIC 25118964 is unusable under the original baseline limit and was excluded without replacement; trials use TIC 287350529 and 160474438 only. Baseline arrays match exactly at both detrending windows. All 16 injections are in range. Short controls give five period/timing matches and four screen passes; long controls give eight period/timing matches and seven passes. All 11 passes meet timing checks. No Earth-radius injection passes. The restricted sample and single phase do not measure survey completeness.

This pilot covers 16 of 1,073 frozen four-sector targets from a 17,324-star metadata pool. The remaining 1,057 selected light curves remain outside this pilot. The recovery searches and repeat off-target source check are reviewed; sensitivity controls remain separate from the original four-sector controls. This does not exhaust TESS or prove a discovery.
