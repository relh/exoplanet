# Remaining coverage after reserving the full Sector 60 sample

The archived metadata inventory has 10,147 unused viable targets after excluding
17,902 distinct prior selections, including every target in the ongoing Sector
60 campaign. The largest overlapping samples are Sector 17 (923), 58 (892),
28 (881), 85 (863) and 1 (855). Counts overlap and cannot be summed.

Raw-query hashes, prior selections and parent pool are checked. Every viable
training sector has at least two other product epochs eight or more sector
numbers away, and previously cached photometry is excluded. No light curve
was opened by this audit. Archive snapshots and dated catalog exclusions limit
coverage; unused here does not prove underexploration, novelty or completeness.
