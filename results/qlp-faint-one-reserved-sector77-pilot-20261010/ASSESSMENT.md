# Sector 77 exploratory search

All 17 frozen targets produced usable searches below the diagnostic screening
threshold. There were no flagged targets or data failures. Neither follow-up
policy found an eligible peak, and reserved sectors remain unopened. No new
planet candidate emerged.

Controls used the first three frozen stars, TIC 287035508, 173166550 and
165760924, without choosing for measured quietness. All baseline arrays agree
exactly at both detrending windows in both control runs. All 24 injections
are within the actual searched period grids. Each control family yielded
three period/phase matches, but none of the 24 trials passed screening.

All short-period trials stayed below the diagnostic threshold. Of the three
long-period matches, one remained below threshold and the other two failed
event-count, odd/even-measurability and alternative-detrending checks; one
also failed event-dominance checks. Original gates are unchanged. No
Earth-radius trial passed. This poor recovery materially limits the null
search result: it cannot be interpreted as evidence that these stars lack
planets. Three stars, one phase and one duration per control family do not
calibrate survey completeness or a false-alarm rate.

The metadata inventory leaves 190 untouched stars in the scoped archive
snapshot. Sector 95 has 16 selected next, with complete historical exclusions,
156 coverage-chunk hashes checked, exact expected target set and zero prior
overlap. Their photometry remains unopened. This search is restricted to the
existing target pool and dated archive snapshot; new to this search is not
proof of novelty or underexploration by other researchers.
