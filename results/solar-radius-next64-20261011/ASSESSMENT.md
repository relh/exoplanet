0 errors and0 eligible follow-up signals**. The156 discovery light-curve products

The highest formal peaks are not reliable planet evidence. For example,
TIC87869692 has a roughly5.4% fitted dip and a formal SNR above2000, but too few
supported events, inconsistent depths, a dominating single event, odd/even mismatch
and a different preferred period under alternative detrending. Its full rejected
fits are retained, without promoting it to a candidate or assigning a physical
classification from those flags alone.

The unchanged search uses two detrending windows, multiple peaks and the existing
signal-consistency/contamination gates. The pilot injection results show these
gates can also reject real injected signals, particularly in variable stars or
with inadequate secondary coverage. This null result is not a planet exclusion
or Earth-radius completeness statement.

error was overlooked. The source snapshot, target-level diagnostics and negative
evidence are retained. No scientific search algorithm changed. No reserved
