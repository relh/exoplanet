## What would justify a new investigation

TIC80387959 and402358350 need convincing host attribution;241928959 needs independent recurrence and companion constraints;469309982 and96166003 need stronger timing/source evidence;5546257 remains contamination-favored with an unresolved physical explanation. None is promoted to PC or CP.

New resolved photometry, genuinely unopened observations under a frozen prediction, physical companion constraints, or a separately validated response method could change these assessments. A non-event pointing correction would first need known-planet and contaminant controls. It is a separate method-development package, not an unfinished candidate fit. Numerical FPP would remain conditional on scenario and instrumental assumptions. Repeating poor image rankings, retuning already opened epochs or retrying failed archive routes without new information is not required to complete this scope.
