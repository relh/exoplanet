# Sector 7: frozen metadata selection

Selected 801 unused stars after excluding all 19,706 earlier selections, including the full ongoing Sector 28 sample. Existing radius/noise ordering, dated catalog exclusions and two reserved sectors at least eight sector numbers away are retained. All archived coverage chunks and parent pool hashes were verified; no selected target had cached photometry. Availability is not proof of novelty or underexploration. A 100-star pilot and deterministic sensitivity controls precede expansion.
