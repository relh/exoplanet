# Sector 91 one-reserved exploratory search

All 463 selected stars produced usable searches: 342 below threshold, 121 flagged,
zero data failures. One original-policy peak (TIC 101436430) and nine two-event
diagnostic peaks on eight additional stars completed frozen-ephemeris follow-up.
Every reserved-sector timing prediction is inconclusive.

TIC 101436430 favors neighboring-source contamination after raw pixel vetting.
The diagnostic group favors shared systematic structure: events following a large
gap pair with nearly coincident later events across widely separated stars.
Individual pixel/catalog vetting of that lower-priority group was not performed.
All ten discovery plots and the main lead's pixel/background plots were inspected.
No securely attributed new planet candidate from this batch.

See the individual assessments and
[group sensitivity diagnostic](../qlp-faint-one-reserved-sector91-gap-diagnostic-20261010/ASSESSMENT.md).
Post-selection diagnostics do not rewrite frozen predictions or screening gates.

Controls on the first three selected stars (319326444, 37147035, 98630189)
include 12 short-period and 12 longer-period injected trials, all within the
actual search grids and using verified identical baseline arrays.
Short trials: seven period/phase recoveries, five passes.
Long trials: seven period/phase recoveries, two passes.
All seven screening passes have valid phase recovery; one is an Earth-radius
trial on TIC 37147035 at 0.83 d. This tiny selected sample is not a completeness
estimate and does not establish sensitivity throughout the search.
