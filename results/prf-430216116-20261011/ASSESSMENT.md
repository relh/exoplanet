# TIC 430216116: strong evidence for a neighboring source

The 4.67-day discovery signal is strongly displaced from TIC 430216116. In all three discovery sectors (43–45), a conditional point-response-function fit favors **TIC 430216137**, T magnitude 15.45, over the bright search target. All 15 individual events and all 15 leave-one-event-out stacks prefer that neighbor among the 12 tested catalog positions. All 27 registration perturbations (nine per sector at offsets −0.1, 0, +0.1 pixels on each axis) also prefer it. Deprioritize the original target as an on-target planet candidate; its earlier 1.69-Earth-radius interpretation is not supported by this localization.

The target-minus-neighbor chi-square differences are approximately 1003, 1725 and 2273. These are conditional fit differences with formal independent pixel errors, not calibrated significances or host probabilities. Best-fit neighbor amplitudes are positive. The observed/model/residual plots were inspected; some residual structure remains, especially near the bright target. The neighbor could itself be blended or variable; these fits do not identify its physical cause.

The model includes T<=18 catalog stars inside the stamp plus a one-pixel margin, with linear proper motions from TIC J2000 positions. Relative scene fluxes are fixed from T magnitudes; a common displacement, normalization and background plane are fitted outside the event windows. Each difference-image hypothesis fits one source plus a plane. Scene RMS is 0.15–0.35% of the peak, and registration stays away from its bounds. Linear proper motion ignores parallax and astrometric uncertainties. The perturbation grid is a sensitivity check, not an uncertainty distribution. No empirical false-localization rate is inferred.

An initial attempt allowing every scene brightness to vary failed during registration; original script, plan and error are preserved in initial-attempt/. The replacement uses catalog flux ratios to avoid that underconstrained fit, with matched-model synthetic shifts recovered to 1e-5 pixels. The unknown catalog/model brightness errors remain a limitation. No event times were retuned for these images.

All five selected Gaia DR2-to-DR3 source mappings reconcile. Gaia returns no variability-classifier or eclipsing-binary row for those sources, including the preferred neighbor; this is not evidence against variability or proof of novelty. The neighbor's catalog stellar parameters and severe dilution mean the old on-target planet-size estimate cannot be transferred to it. No new planet around the neighbor is claimed.

Verification reconstructs all five target/sector pairs across this and the companion pixel analysis from hashed FITS data. For this PRF analysis, 396 stacked, individual-event and omission fits are independently reconstructed with a QR solver. Raw pixel and mission PRF hashes are checked. The existing 49-test suite passed in 28.06 seconds. Saved image templates/weights and full negative evidence are retained.
