## Recommended order and experiments

1. **Reliability controls for96166003 and469309982.** These are lower-significance leads with mixed timing evidence, so deciding whether their peaks are exceptional is more useful than more source-template permutations. First reproduce each actual selection statistic and search neighborhood. For96166003 that is the original43/44 search;469309982 came from a weak-seed local refinement and needs that procedure represented. Freeze deterministic null settings and additive-signal recovery controls before execution. The existing [362222987 control](../null-362222987-20261011/ASSESSMENT.md) is a useful implementation pattern but tests maximum search power, **not** complete downstream gates or survey false-alarm probability. A full-selection interpretation requires actually rerunning the relevant selection stages. One fixed control matrix per target is enough to inform priority; favorable results retain uncertainty, unfavorable results may justify deprioritization.

2. **Conditional physical compatibility for241928959.** Its~1.1% depth and~0.032day box duration are worth confronting with an exposure-integrated transit model, actual cadence, catalog stellar errors and plausible grazing/dilution alternatives. The [source result](../prf-fullscene-241928959-20261011/ASSESSMENT.md) is more adequate than the shallow poor-residual cases, but it cannot exclude an unresolved binary. The close neighbor requires a substantial eclipse under the existing illustrative flux ratio. Preserve the uncertain [photometric youth](../spyglass-241928959-20261011/ASSESSMENT.md), one-odd-event [parity limitation](../parity-241928959-20261011/ASSESSMENT.md), and [half-phase limits](../secondary-241928959-20261011/ASSESSMENT.md). Compatibility is not validation; a circular-model tension alone is not a binary verdict. The [402358350 model](../physical-402358350-20261011/ASSESSMENT.md) supplies reusable machinery and a warning about local minima and density/impact degeneracy. Stop after a declared comparison grid if both interpretations remain viable.

3. **One empirical-source feasibility gate for80387959/402358350.** Their bottleneck is inaccurate or non-identifying image response. The [80387959 event controls](../prf-80387959-event-controls-20261011/ASSESSMENT.md), [gradient test](../prf-80387959-motion-sensitivity-20261011/ASSESSMENT.md) and [402358350 sector81](../prf-402358350-s81-20261011/ASSESSMENT.md) already show that more nuisance terms/lowerχ² do not establish a host. First assess whether existing stamps contain enough out-of-event scene diversity to constrain a response independently of the candidate event. Train and assess on disjoint non-event data; require meaningful held-out residual and known-planet/contaminant control performance before candidate application. Small crowded stamps may make this impossible. That is a valid no-go outcome, not an invitation to another round of unconstrained fits. A large new calibrated-imagery project is separate scope, not a hidden completion requirement.

4. **Conditional public-metadata work.** ASAS-SN epoch coverage remains unknown for241928959 and402358350; old variable-catalog nonmatches did not query its master photometry catalog. However the [241928959 coverage audit](../coverage-review-241928959-20261011/ASSESSMENT.md) records refused service connections. Retry only with evidence of a working documented route, then check association/cadence/sensitivity before flux. [402358350 ZTF/Gaia](../external-coverage-402358350-20261011/ASSESSMENT.md) does not identify a target light curve. Repeating those exact metadata queries is not useful unless coverage changes. Complete a dated exact-identity/CTOI/literature consolidation for still-viable leads only where missing; the recent402358350 novelty report is already a model for this. Never equate a nonmatch with being undiscovered.
