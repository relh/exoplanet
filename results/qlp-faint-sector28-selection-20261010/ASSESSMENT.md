# Sector 28: frozen metadata selection

Selected 881 unused stars after excluding all 18,825 prior selections, including the complete Sector 17 selection. Retained the existing radius/noise ranking, dated catalog exclusions and requirement for at least two reserved sectors at least eight sector numbers away. All 156 archived coverage chunks and pool hashes were verified. No selected star had cached target photometry. Availability and being new to this search do not establish novelty or underexploration.

A 100-star pilot and deterministic short/long-period controls precede expansion. Search bounds, failed products and follow-up source attribution must be reviewed.
