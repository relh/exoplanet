# TIC 147201125: contamination from a known neighboring eclipsing binary

The visually inspected QLP28 plot shows ten deep V-shaped dips. Refined period
1.880176854 days, duration 0.18144 days and epoch BTJD 2063.102693820 give a
native fixed depth of 35,703 ± 301 ppm. The exploratory half-phase scan selects
1,589 ± 488 ppm, insufficient alone to assign a binary classification. The
original crowding flag remains.

Frozen checks in Sectors 1 and 68 give 13,240 ± 329 and −1,312 ± 267 ppm.
Timing spreads of 0.5318 and 0.7917 days exceed the dip duration; both checks
remain timing-inconclusive, including the strong Sector 1 signal. SPOC28 has a
much smaller same-epoch depth, 6,195 ± 200 ppm. QLP and SPOC share photons.

The original 11×11 TESScut image shows a compact off-target source clipped at
the bottom edge. A separately preserved 21×21 image contains it completely;
both were visually inspected. Nine generic pixel windows yield a formal peak
SNR 8015 and difference moment 5.440 pixels off target, only 0.147 pixels from
TIC 147201138 (T=10.97). Formal pixel errors and moments are not calibrated PRF
confidence intervals; nearby blends and catalog motion remain limitations.

TIC 147201138 maps to Gaia DR3 6576042981165197696, listed as an eclipsing
binary with period 1.880491587 days. The TESS period differs by −0.01674 percent.
Three neighboring stars were mapped; one classifier and one binary row returned.
At the unchanged target timing, that neighbor's QLP28 has a 61.819 ± 0.156 percent
primary and 11.189 ± 0.177 percent half-phase dip. QLP1 has 14.468 ± 0.265 and
3.259 ± 0.279 percent respectively, with the same long-baseline timing caveat.
Neighboring apertures share image information; this is not independent validation.

Disposition: strong evidence for contamination from the known neighboring binary
TIC 147201138, not a new planet around TIC 147201125. Sector 95 remains unopened
as target photometry. No external submissions or contacts.
