# Visual audit of selected flagged Sector 32 signals

Both native-cadence discovery plots were viewed. These are post-selection
diagnostics using training data only; no reserved epochs or thresholds changed.

TIC 1357792: the 0.904697 d fold has broad, smooth oscillations with troughs
and crests of comparable magnitude, not an isolated transit shape. Sinusoidal,
secondary/variability, inversion, duty-cycle and correlated-noise flags remain.
Its original variability also remains in injection controls.

TIC 372953580: the formal SNR ~573 peak at 2.035909 d shows very deep,
alternating eclipselike events, reaching roughly 35% flux loss. The odd/even
mismatch and inconsistent-depth flags are supported by the plot; the ~4.07228 d
harmonic also has secondary/variability flags. This favors an eclipsing stellar
system over a planet transit, but neither the physical host nor a fundamental
orbital period has been established here. No novelty or target attribution claim.
It is retained as a flagged signal, not promoted to a planet candidate.

The manifest links source hashes and plot hashes. High formal SNR alone does
not override the screening failures.
