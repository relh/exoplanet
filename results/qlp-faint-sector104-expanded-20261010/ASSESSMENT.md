# Sector 104 extension complete: two unpromoted leads

All 1,855 planned expansion targets finished: 1,682 below threshold, 172
flagged, and one original data failure. A separate Sector 103 recovery of
the failed target, TIC 260756859, completed below threshold. Thus all 1,855
distinct expansion targets have a usable search, although one was not usable
in Sector 104. The separate 100-star pilot also completed without a qualifying
lead. These 1,955 stars are disjoint from the previous faint-star selections.

One original-policy follow-up, [TIC 306101698](../qlp-faint-sector104-followup-20261010/306101698-peak0/ASSESSMENT.md),
has a 2.870-day signal but inconclusive reserved timing and a Sector 104 pixel
offset toward nearby stars. The additional Sector 103 pixel check is unusable
because broad negative background structure dominates it. Host attribution
and a planetary explanation remain unestablished.

One coverage-diagnostic follow-up, [TIC 201228641](../qlp-faint-sector104-coverage-followup-20261010/201228641-peak0/ASSESSMENT.md),
has an 8.660-day peak supported by only two discovery events. Broad timing
uncertainty and structured flux leave it unsupported. It never passed the
original event-count gate. Neither lead is promoted to a likely new planet.

Discovery and both watchers exited successfully. [Reconciliation](reconciliation.json)
checks all target results against the frozen plan, raw hashes, the explicitly
preserved error and recovery, both follow-up inventories, and completed plots,
measurements and assessments. [audit.json](audit.json) retains all selected
top-three peaks, including original flags. Missing SPOC files, the failed
neighbor-query attempt, and the failed adjacent-sector image are retained.

This is a limited search of metadata-ranked faint nearby small stars. Periods
run from 0.5 days to the smaller of 20 days and clean baseline/2.5, with ten-minute
bins and unchanged detrending and quality gates. The [pilot controls](../qlp-faint-sector104-pilot-20261010/ASSESSMENT.md)
show incomplete recovery, and some initial injections fell outside the actual
search range. Those outcomes are preserved; additional in-range controls do
not replace them. These checks do not establish completeness, a calibrated
false-positive rate, planet exclusions, or novelty. No external submissions
or contacts were made.
