# Two-sector outer-distance pilot

TIC 406767596 and TIC 376405406 both yielded usable searches of discovery sectors 84–85, below the screening threshold. Neither follow-up policy found an eligible peak. All three and five reserved light curves respectively remain unopened. No new planet candidate emerged.

Controls used both frozen stars. Baseline arrays agree exactly at both detrending windows, and all 16 injections lie within the actual period grids. Short controls yield four period/timing recoveries and four screen passes: two Earth radii at 0.83 and 2.37 days in each star. Long controls yield eight period/timing matches and five passes: two and three Earth radii at 5.73 days in each star, plus three Earth radii at 8.37 days in TIC 376405406. All nine passing trials satisfy timing checks. No Earth-radius injection passes. These single-phase controls do not measure completeness or exclude small planets.

Seven untouched pool stars have a single discovery sector and at least two reserved sectors separated by eight sector numbers or more. Their metadata splits are frozen and verified before opening photometry. Shorter discovery baselines weaken long-period sensitivity; original screening gates remain unchanged. Four other pool stars do not satisfy this rule. Catalog nonmatches do not prove novelty.
