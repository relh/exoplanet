zero failures. Neither follow-up policy admitted a peak. Terminal inventories
and source hashes reconcile; all reserved epochs remain unopened.

[Strong-dip review](../qlp-faint-one-reserved-sector30-flagged-review-20261010/ASSESSMENT.md)
finds six nearby Camera 1 / CCD 1 stars with large deficits restricted to a
shared interval. Their periods fail repetition/consistency checks. A shared
observing or processing disturbance is favored, without claiming a physical
cause or excluding every isolated event. A failed initial interval comparison
and its explicit corrected version are both retained.

Controls used the first three frozen stars (32208408, 3887989, 423665356):
all 24 trials lie within their actual search grids; baseline arrays match exactly
for both detrending windows. Short trials: seven period/phase recoveries and
five screening passes. Longer trials: eight period recoveries, seven phase
recoveries, four screening passes. All nine passes have valid phase recovery;
one is an Earth-radius trial on TIC 32208408 at 0.83 d. Period recovery alone
is not sufficient. This small control sample is not a completeness estimate,
and no qualifying signal does not imply no planets.

the scoped one-distant-reserved-sector pool. Sector 47 is selected next with
