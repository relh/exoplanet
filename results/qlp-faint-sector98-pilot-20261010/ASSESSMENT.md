# Sector 98 faint-star pilot

All 100 frozen targets completed: 73 below the diagnostic screening threshold,
27 flagged, no errors, and no peak eligible for original or diagnostic-only
follow-up. All three selected periodogram peaks per star remain in the audit.
No reserved sector was opened by this pilot or its injections.

The metadata selection contains 1,535 stars outside the previous 1,945-star
four-sector selection, each with Sector 98 and at least two reserved sectors.
It retains the existing faint-star radius/noise ordering and dated catalog
exclusions. The metadata source hashes and exclusion counts are in
[the selection audit](../qlp-faint-sector98-selection-20261010/audit.json).
New to this investigation does not mean unstudied or undiscovered.

The unchanged search uses ten-minute QLP bins, periods from 0.5 days to the
smaller of 20 days and clean baseline/2.5, and the existing detrending and
signal-quality gates. Large formal peaks failed checks including variability,
inverted signals, event consistency, and correlated noise. This pilot does
not exclude planets outside its search grid or below its sensitivity.

[Twelve deterministic raw-flux injections](../qlp-faint-sector98-injections-20261010/ASSESSMENT.md)
recovered their periods and checked phases, with eight passing gates apart
from unmeasured crowding. They provide a limited sensitivity check, not survey
completeness or calibrated false-positive probabilities. The remaining 1,435
targets are being searched separately with the same pipeline.
