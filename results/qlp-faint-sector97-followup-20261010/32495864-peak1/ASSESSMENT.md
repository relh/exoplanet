# TIC 32495864: unresolved long-period signal

Not yet a likely planet candidate. The 13.815568663-day native fit (duration
0.18144 days) passes the initial filters except unmeasured crowding. At frozen
timing, depth is 2,322 ± 280 ppm, formal SNR 8.29, with four supported events.
The inspected light curve shows broad depressions and gaps. A competing
14.360319676-day fit has only two supported events; neither fit is established
as a unique physical period.

Reserved Sectors 3 and 30 give no adequate fixed-window total measurement,
but timing spreads 2.58 and 1.86 days prevent confirmation or exclusion.
Same-epoch binned QLP yields 2,230 ± 288 ppm (SNR 7.73). The attempted TESS-SPOC
product returns HTTP 404, not a nondetection. Selected off-phase dip and
brightening have SNR 3.25 and −4.82; these are uncalibrated searches.

The raw pixel difference is background dominated and cannot localize a source.
After a post-selection spatial plane subtraction at two exclusion radii, a
compact residual lies near the target. This is encouraging for source review
but does not prove a transit or remove instrumental explanations. No catalog
novelty claim or secure host attribution is made. Further epoch and
background/shape checks are needed before promotion.
