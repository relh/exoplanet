# Sector 17: frozen metadata selection

Selected 923 unused stars after excluding all 17,902 earlier selections,
including the entire ongoing Sector 60 sample. Existing radius/noise ordering
and dated catalog exclusions are retained. Every target has at least two
reserved product epochs eight or more sector numbers away. No target photometry
was previously cached. Hashes verify the parent pool and archived query chunks.

Sector 17 has the largest remaining viable sample in the archived coverage
audit. This establishes availability, not novelty or underexploration. A 100-star
pilot and deterministic controls precede any expansion.
