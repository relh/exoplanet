# Remaining coverage after reserving the full Sector 17 sample

The metadata inventory has 9,224 unused viable stars after excluding 18,825
previously selected targets, including all 923 Sector 17 selections. Largest
overlapping samples: Sector 28 (881), 1 (855), 95 (827), 68 (823), 7 (801).
Counts overlap and must not be summed. No photometry was opened by the audit.

All query chunks, parent pool and prior selection hashes are checked. Each
viable training sector has two product epochs eight or more sector numbers
away; cached photometry is excluded. Archived metadata and dated catalog
exclusions limit the inventory. It establishes neither novelty nor completeness.
