# Faint stars with radii above the previous small-star limit

This metadata extension covers 0.5 < radius <= 0.8 solar, 12 < Tmag <= 13.5, distance <= 120 pc, temperature <= 5200 K and catalog contamination ratio <= 0.2. Previous exact catalog IDs, selected targets and opened light curves are excluded. The previous faint pool stopped at 0.5 solar radii. Larger hosts give shallower transits at fixed planet size; the extension is exploratory and requires sensitivity controls.

Four distance-partitioned catalog queries completed with 24,973 distinct rows. The frozen pool contains 17,324 stars after excluding 7,448 for crowding, 114 known/opened targets, one temperature failure, six boundary overlaps and 80 catalog dispositions. All exclusion sources and query provenance are retained. Catalog nonmatches and metadata ranking do not establish novelty or measured quietness.

QLP coverage queries completed for the entire pool, with products for 16,924 targets. A metadata-only rule selected 1,073 targets with four consecutive discovery sectors and at least two reserved sectors each eight sector numbers or more from discovery. A 16-star pilot uses the first targets in radius/brightness ranking. All its QLP photometry was absent when that pilot was frozen. The remaining 1,057 selected targets have not been searched in this pilot.

Consecutive sector numbers do not guarantee a <=120-day clean baseline. Five pilot targets fail that guard; the original failures are retained and reduced-window recovery plans were frozen using only time coverage, with reserved sectors unchanged. This is not a change to scientific screening gates or an assertion of survey completeness.
