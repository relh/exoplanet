# Bright small stars beyond the original distance limit

This metadata-only extension covers 120 < distance <= 200 pc, 6 <= Tmag <= 12, and 0.15 <= radius <= 0.6 solar. It favors brighter targets while extending beyond the previous 120-pc pool. Catalog radius and brightness are sensitivity proxies, not observed quietness or proof that stars are underexplored.

Two distance-partitioned MAST queries completed with 242 distinct catalog rows. Local cuts reject 162 for temperature above 4200 K or missing temperature, 63 for missing/high catalog contamination ratio (>0.2), and two for exact dated catalog IDs or previously opened/selected targets. Fifteen remain, all with QLP metadata. Every earlier selected-ID source and catalog exclusion is recorded and hashed. Catalog nonmatches do not establish novelty; source and catalog checks remain necessary for any prospective candidate.

The initial frozen rule requires four consecutive discovery sectors and at least two reserved sectors each at least eight sector numbers from every discovery sector. TIC 239124404 qualifies with discovery sectors 44–47 and reserved sectors 20 and 60. Its light curves were opened only after freezing this split.

After excluding that target, a separate metadata-only three-consecutive-sector rule selects TIC 392763403 with discovery sectors 101–103 and reserved sectors 13, 27, 67 and 93. Its QLP photometry remained unopened when verified. Thirteen other pool stars do not satisfy either rule. This does not establish an exhaustive TESS search or survey completeness.
