# TIC 16488471: likely contamination near a known binary

Native period 2.136846914 days, duration 0.1104 days, fixed QLP depth
2,748 ± 203 ppm (SNR 13.51), nine supported events. The discovery plot was
inspected. Reserved Sectors 44/45 have SNR 0.78/1.25, but timing spreads
0.528/0.512 day make these inconclusive, not exclusions.

The inspected Sector 72 difference image has ten usable windows and a compact
positive source 2.111 pixels off-target, 0.166 pixel from TIC 16488473. A second
pixel epoch, Sector 71, uses the unchanged Sector 72 timing: nine windows,
2.180-pixel target offset and 0.139-pixel distance to the same neighbor.
Both plots were inspected. Formal peak pixel SNRs 51.25/27.50 and moments
are not calibrated positional probabilities or proof of a unique component.

TIC 16488473 maps to Gaia DR3 3164881754549276800, listed as an eclipsing binary.
Its catalog period is 2.172575519 days, differing from the TESS fit by
-1.645%. The mismatch is explicit: this is spatial evidence
near a known binary, not an exact period/epoch identification. Another faint
source TIC 761888223 lies 0.726/0.592 pixel from the two moments. Exact source
assignment remains uncertain. No neighbor QLP products were returned for the
requested sectors; missing products are not nondetections.

Same-epoch binned QLP gives 2,629 ± 209 ppm, versus TESS-SPOC 546 ± 181 ppm.
The substantially weaker signal in another aperture/reduction supports
contamination, but both use the same photons. Not promoted to a planet.
