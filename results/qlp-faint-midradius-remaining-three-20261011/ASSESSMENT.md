# Running search of the remaining 1,493 three-sector targets

This campaign follows the reviewed 16-star pilot and searches every remaining star in the frozen 1,509-star three-sector selection. It is disjoint from the entire four-sector tier, including that tier's currently unprocessed targets. All selected QLP cache paths were absent when this campaign was frozen. Three consecutive discovery sectors and at least two distant reserved sectors are retained for each star.

The existing search, screening gates, 120-day baseline guard and four-worker limit are unchanged. Two follow-up monitors apply the original and coverage-diagnostic policies. A separate recovery stream preserves original failures and selects shorter windows using time coverage only; nonbaseline errors require individual review. No newly generated signal or plot is automatically promoted to a planet candidate.

The preceding pilot's controls passed seven of 24 injections through both screening and timing checks, at two or three Earth radii. No Earth-radius trial passed. Those few stars and single injected phase/duration per family do not establish sensitivity or completeness for this much larger sample. This is an ongoing scoped search, not whole-TESS coverage or proof of novelty.

The first immutable checkpoint covers 48 completed searches: 38 below threshold and ten flagged, with no errors. TIC 355707138 is admitted only as an alternate-detrending diagnostic, not a full screening pass. Its reserved timing is inconclusive and its source checks remain pending. This partial snapshot does not establish the outcome for the planned 1,493-star campaign.
