# Two retrospective signals: raw-event diagnostics

All four requested 11x11 TESScut products downloaded and passed source-hash,
FITS-integrity and sector/time-reference checks. Each contains one supported
event at the unchanged frozen timing. We retain individual event maps rather
than align and coadd sectors into an unjustified localization.

All four maps were visually reviewed. TIC352831349 shows broad positive
background changes across the stamps in both Sectors16 and17, including
column-like structure. TIC258513090 is entirely negative in the Sector85
baseline-minus-event image and broadly positive in Sector86. None establishes
a repeatable compact dimming source at the catalog target.

These maps are uncorrected for spatial background. A negative whole-stamp
difference cannot locate a dimming star; a broad positive one cannot be read
as a stellar centroid. The patterns support an instrumental/background concern,
but do not uniquely determine the physical cause or exclude an underlying
transit. No formal localization or significance is claimed. Separate processed
products were unavailable at the requested URLs, not astrophysical nulls.

Both signals remain artifact-suspect diagnostics, not credible planet
candidates. Existing reserved-sector timing remains inconclusive. All source
URLs, hashes, counts and maps are retained. No event requirement was relaxed
to promote a host, and no new ephemeris was fitted.
