# Alternate data for the six original failures

All first 5,000 selected stars now have at least one usable discovery search:
**4,996 in SPOC and four only in QLP**. This does not erase the six failures in
the original 5,000-target checkpoint or imply uniform sensitivity. No new
review priority emerged. `checkpoint-5000-recovery-20261009.json` links the
original counts to each additional result with hashes.

The selection and product availability were recorded before searching:

- TIC 268319776, 307440881, 199608520 and 383742525 have QLP observations of
  their original discovery sectors. All four alternative-product searches
  completed. The first, third and fourth have strongest S/N below 7. The
  strongest peak for 307440881 has fewer than three supported events, cannot
  measure odd/even or event consistency, and changes period with detrending.
  It is not a credible periodic transit detection despite its high BLS statistic.
- TIC 176954932 and 149251611 lack original Sector 1 archive products, and QLP
  also lacks complete coverage of their original Sector 1/2/3 combination.
  The earliest fully listed consecutive triple from Sector 4 onward was selected
  without inspecting its flux: Sectors 4/5/6 for both targets. These SPOC searches
  completed with strongest S/N 5.89 and 5.19 respectively.

Reserved sectors were not opened. QLP missing crowding remains unassessed.
Empirical errors were permitted only for a sector with no usable published
errors, and the per-sector evidence identifies the actual error model used.
Companion exposure-model selection checks add no priority for either run.

These are recovery searches with different data, not retroactive success of
the original failed inputs. The original and alternative products, sector
choices, errors and source hashes remain available for audit.
