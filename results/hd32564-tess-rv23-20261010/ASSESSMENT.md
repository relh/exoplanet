# HD 32564: targeted 23.597-day transit check

This post-hoc native-QLP search fixes the rounded external RV period to23.597days
and searches all phases over box durations0.04,0.08,0.12,0.18,0.24days in Sector98.
It makes no RV conjunction-time assumption and does not scan RV period uncertainty.
The original blind-search results are retained separately.

The selected BLS maximum has depth173 ± 67ppm (formal SNR2.58), duration0.18days,
and epoch BTJD4006.4084269222. The fixed local-baseline measurement is167 ± 75ppm
(SNR2.24), with only one usable event at BTJD4030.0054269222. Thus the49.27day
cleaned time span does not translate to two usable events at this selected phase.
This selected weak dip is not a transit candidate or calibrated significance.

At the unchanged selected model, already-opened Sector5 has no usable local
event measurement, and Sector32 gives10 ± 47ppm (SNR0.22). These are post-hoc
comparisons, not blind validation. Period uncertainty over the multi-year gap
was not quantified, so they cannot decisively reject the external RV hypothesis.

No qualifying transit signal is established. No injection-calibrated depth,
radius, orientation or planet exclusion is supported. Long-baseline timestamp
coverage must not be substituted for actual event coverage. Raw provenance,
selected model and all weak or missing measurements are retained.
