# Sector 95 exploratory search

All 16 frozen targets produced usable searches below the diagnostic threshold.
There were no flagged targets or data failures. Neither follow-up policy found
an eligible peak, and reserved sectors remain unopened. No new planet
candidate emerged.

Controls used the first three frozen stars, TIC 92876924, 369219005 and
441201780, without selecting by measured quietness. All baseline arrays agree
exactly at both detrending windows in both control runs. Short-period controls
yielded eight period/phase matches and three screening passes: all three stars
at 0.83 days and two Earth radii. All three passes satisfy the phase check;
no Earth-radius trial passed. All twelve short-period trials are in range.

The control stars have clean baselines of approximately 8.50 days, limiting
the actual search grids to about 3.40 days. All twelve longer-period trials
(5.73 and 8.37 days) are outside these grids. None recovered the injected
period; these trials cannot be counted as in-range sensitivity failures.
Any harmonic peaks are not recoveries of the injected period. Combined,
three of twelve in-range trials passed screening and timing. The short
baseline limits the null result, and this search does not exclude longer
period planets. Three stars, one phase and one duration per control family
do not establish survey completeness or a false-alarm rate.

The metadata inventory leaves 174 untouched stars in the scoped archive
snapshot. Sector 24 has 15 selected next, with complete historical exclusions,
156 coverage-chunk hashes checked, exact expected set and zero prior overlap.
Their photometry remains unopened. New to this search is not proof of novelty
or underexploration by other researchers. Scope remains the existing target
pool and dated archive snapshot, not an exhaustive search of all TESS data.
