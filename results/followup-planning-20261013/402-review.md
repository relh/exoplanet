# Independent review of TIC 74037550 archival follow-up

**No blocking finding. All 72 comparisons are correctly retained as inconclusive; the report does not establish or imply a source exclusion.** Read review covered the final assessment, frozen plan, analysis, raw-export verifier and summary. [402-review.json](402-review.json) records the reviewed file hashes and 498 independent arithmetic checks; [review402.py](review402.py) reproduces them offline.

The six streams contain 836 raw and 545 selected measurements. Six individual streams plus three combined filters, four preserved clocks and two time-scale interpretations produce 72 unique comparisons. Every comparison fails its saved timing and/or event-count criterion. The expanded primary combined g/r/i results reproduce the report: 3 samples in two cycles, one sample in one cycle and zero samples, respectively. The primary timing spreads exceed half the 0.144-day duration, even before adding time-scale ambiguity. Thus the nominally adequate g/r depth sensitivity cannot justify excluding an eclipse.

Independent weighted nuisance projection reproduces fitted decrements, covariance errors and scatter-scaled errors without using the original least-squares or verifier QR implementation. The largest depth difference is below 4e-16; the largest scaled-error difference is below 4e-18. Independent endpoint-clock propagation agrees within 1e-9 day. The maximum absolute formal depth/error ratio is 1.69145, consistent with the report's less-than-1.70 statement. These are arithmetic comparisons, not significance calibrations.

The verifier separately reconstructs raw-export quality masks, flux conversion, iterative heliocentric-to-barycentric times, deterministic duplicate handling, event counts and QR fits. It checks that the frozen plan predates retrieval and retains all selected object IDs. The coordinator reruns its 563 checks. This review does not repeat the downloads or claim a second independent time-standard calibration.

The 13.09% illustrative eclipse comes from a single-sector modeled aperture fraction; it is not an observed eclipse or robust minimum. The report appropriately qualifies the g/r sensitivity ratios by timing and color assumptions, retains the isolated low point without fitting a favorable alternative clock, and acknowledges possible unresolved blending. Archive access errors remain errors rather than non-detections. No change to candidate classification, novelty or calibrated probability is justified.
