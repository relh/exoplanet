# TIC 31869709: training-only shorter-period check

This post-hoc check uses only already-opened QLP Sectors 27–30. It retains the
frozen epoch and duration and tests P/3 = 0.3767365160 days without optimization.
At the original 1.1302095480-day period, fixed phases 0, 1/3 and 2/3 give
2,548 ± 308, 1,773 ± 308 and 2,070 ± 315 ppm respectively. All three phase
measurements are positive in every sector, although some are weak.

At P/3, the pooled depth is 2,133 ± 179 ppm (formal SNR 11.91; 1,065 in-event
points). Sector 27 measures 1,668 ± 341 ppm, 28 measures 2,214 ± 359 ppm,
29 measures 2,781 ± 383 ppm and 30 measures 2,005 ± 354 ppm. The local baseline
window at P/3 does not overlap adjacent predicted events.

This supports a shorter repeating signal family near 0.3767 days. It does not
resolve a planet versus eclipsing binary, contamination or processing artifact.
The original detrending failure remains, the test was selected after inspecting
the same training data, and formal SNR is not calibrated discovery significance.
No reserved data were opened or used for fitting. The 1.130-day reserved tests
cannot be represented as validation of a newly selected shorter-period model.

A dedicated shorter-period refinement and frozen prediction check are warranted
before dismissing or promoting the signal. Host attribution and catalog checks
would still be needed if it survives. The existing unpromoted status remains.
