# Next256 solar-radius targets

Ranks81–336 of the frozen7,004-star eligibility list are selected, without
changing ranking or screening thresholds. All selected discovery and reserved
SPOC cache files were absent before launch. The plan records the exact ranking
slice and hashes before any photometry was downloaded.

Discovery uses the existing two/three adjacent early sectors; each later sector
remains reserved. FITS target identity, checksum and useful baseline are checked
by the loader. Known-object exclusions are cached exact-TIC matches, not a claim
of novelty. Any qualifying peak needs independent-epoch recovery and source/
catalog checks. The pilot injection limitations continue to apply.

The discovery run is separate in `results/solar-radius-next256-20261011`.
Its authoritative terminal status and per-target records must be reconciled
before a final batch result is claimed. No candidate is inferred from selection.
