## Proposed message

**Subject: T17 feasibility for resolved differential photometry of TIC 80387959**

Hello,

We are assessing T17 for differential photometry of TIC 80387959 and nearby
stars. We are investigating an unconfirmed transit-like signal and need to
measure the target and neighboring sources separately.

The approximate J2027 finder position is RA 165.310685°, Dec −48.614053°.
The target has TESS magnitude 10.52; the principal neighboring source of interest is
TIC 80387965, approximately 14 arcseconds away, with TESS magnitude 13.62.
These are TESS magnitudes, not magnitudes in your filters.

One provisional observing interval is **28 March 2027, 12:05–17:43 UTC**,
including the timing uncertainty, expected event and baseline on each side.
It is a geometry proposal, not a booking request. Could you confirm:

1. Whether T17 can track this field throughout that interval, accounting for
   the actual azimuth-dependent horizon, mount limits, meridian flip and any
   scheduled interruptions. Please indicate expected flip/readout gaps.
2. The current camera, gain, filters, linearity/saturation limits and supported
   exposure lengths. The public page lists calibrated darks for 240 seconds;
   are matching calibrations available for shorter exposures that keep a
   magnitude-10.5 target unsaturated?
3. Whether full calibrated images, raw frames, masks and calibration files are
   available, with documented exposure timestamps and time standard, for
   independent photometric extraction.
4. Whether a short preliminary sequence could establish source separation,
   saturation limits and differential-photometry noise before committing to
   an event observation. We do not assume the telescope can measure a shallow
   target transit from its published specifications alone.
5. The permitted manual reservation workflow, maximum continuous reservation,
   scheduling lead time, and a current quote for a preliminary sequence and
   one approximately 5.6-hour reserved session, including overheads, account
   requirements and weather cancellation terms.

No observations or purchases are authorized by this inquiry. We would review
feasibility and cost before deciding whether to request telescope time.

Thank you.

## Decisions after a reply

A useful reply must establish instrument-specific access and tracking, not only
confirm that T17 exists. Preserve the reply as external evidence. An observing
trial would need a separate frozen exposure/calibration and sensitivity plan,
user authorization for any charge or booking, and successful unsaturated source
measurements. A favorable operator response is not demonstrated photometric
precision; neither a trial nor one localized dip completes the planetary-origin
goal. The second candidate still needs ephemeris recovery.

No price or availability is inferred from the public documentation. The user
can instead provide an existing telescope's site, camera, filters and access
constraints; the same scientific requirements apply.
