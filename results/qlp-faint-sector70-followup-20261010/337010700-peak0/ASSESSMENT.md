# TIC 337010700: likely neighbor eclipsing-binary contamination

The native 1.407785030-day signal has duration 0.05952 days, fixed depth
6,276 ± 214 ppm and 15 supported events. The inspected discovery plot has
clear repeated dips. However, the inspected Sector 70 difference image has a
compact positive source 3.179 pixels from the target and only 0.085 pixel
from TIC 337010705. Formal peak pixel SNR is 385 across 16 usable windows;
this is not a calibrated positional confidence interval.

At unchanged timing and duration, TIC 337010705's QLP extraction measures
29.380 ± 0.143% eclipses in Sector 70. This strongly favors contamination by
a neighboring eclipsing binary. The exact host is not formally resolved at
TESS resolution. Neighbor Sector 43 does not reproduce the fixed window, but
the target's frozen prediction spread there is 0.123 day versus a 0.0595-day
duration, so this is inconclusive. Target reserved Sectors 42 and 43 similarly
have negative fixed-window depths with imprecise timing, not exclusions.

Same-epoch binned target QLP gives 6,194 ± 214 ppm, while TESS-SPOC gives
20 ± 191 ppm. Different reductions of the same images are not independent
observations, but this contrast further supports aperture contamination.
A Gaia DR2-to-DR3 mapping for TIC 337010705 returned one source and no
classifier or eclipsing-binary rows; absence of a label is not evidence of
novelty or nonvariability. Not promoted to a planet candidate.
