# TIC 304608681: likely off-target contamination

The discovery plot was inspected: a dip is visible in the folded light curve,
but source diagnostics favor a nearby star. Native QLP refinement gives
P=2.5743413042 days, epoch BTJD 2799.4056517250, duration 0.048 days and BLS
depth 6,738±731 ppm. The local-depth measurement is 7,674±805 ppm with seven
individually supported events.

Frozen predictions in sectors 14 and 41 measure 259±1,323 and −1,210±786 ppm.
Timing spreads of 0.7871 and 0.2845 days exceed the duration, so neither test
establishes recurrence or excludes a dip at uncertain timing. The exploratory
off-phase scan gives a selected SNR 2.65, not a calibrated false-alarm probability.

Same-sector TESS-SPOC measures −24,920±795 ppm at the fixed ephemeris, opposite
in sign to QLP. Its reported aperture crowding is 0.608. This is strong reduction
dependence, not independent astrophysical confirmation; both use the same photons.

The Sector 55 difference image was inspected. Ten generic event windows produce
a compact positive source about 4.066 pixels from the target, near TIC 304608654
(0.191 pixels from the descriptive moment). Peak formal pixel SNR is 159.56.
This supports off-target contamination; raw-image moments are not calibrated PRF
confidence and do not uniquely identify a component. Fainter TIC 1945927687 is
also close to the localized region.

Three TIC Gaia identifiers map to three DR3 sources. No eclipsing-binary rows
were returned. The target itself has a SOLAR_LIKE classifier row with score 0.099;
that label does not identify the contaminating source or validate the period.
The bright neighboring TIC 304608654 has no returned QLP products in sectors
55 or 14, so direct light-curve verification is unavailable.

Disposition: likely neighboring-source contamination with strong processing
dependence; exact component and physical period remain unresolved. No planet
candidate is promoted. Reserved target Sector 81 remains unopened. Original
plots, measurements, nonmatches and missing-product evidence are retained.
