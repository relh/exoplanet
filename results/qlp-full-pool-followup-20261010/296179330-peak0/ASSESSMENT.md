# TIC296179330: previously published candidate signal, not a new discovery

The~0.6884d QLP signal is already represented in the
[DTARPS Galactic-plane list (Melton et al.2024, Table4)](https://cdsarc.cds.unistra.fr/viz-bin/cat/J/AJ/167/203),
row239, at0.68825d and depth0.0008. The retrieved complete table, exact TIC match
and source hashes are in `../../new-qlp-lead-catalogs-20261010`.

Our native Sector33/34 refinement gives0.6883976d, duration0.032d and1,319±62ppm.
Frozen tests in7/87 do not show a dip at nominal phase, but timing spreads
0.115/0.230d exceed half the duration, so those tests remain inconclusive.
This is not an independently validated planet and not our discovery.

The paper's narrative distance note differs from current Gaia DR3. The matching
DR3 source3032685482982244096 has parallax20.8408±0.0247mas, consistent with the
TIC~48pc distance. Do not adopt the narrative~430pc distance or its implied
large planet radius without resolving the discrepancy. Gaia/TIC identity and
photometric source attribution are separate issues; faint neighbors exist.
