# Next64 solar-radius targets

Ranks17–80 of the previously frozen7,004-star eligibility list form this batch.
The radius/brightness ranking, catalog exclusions, contamination threshold,
contiguous discovery sectors and reserved later sectors are unchanged. Every
selected discovery and reserved SPOC file was absent before launching the run.

`plan.json` records source/selection hashes and the exact slice before photometry
was opened. This is an exploratory extension following the pilot recovery tests;
those tests do not establish Earth-size completeness. Strong peaks still require
all screening gates, independent-sector recovery and source/catalog vetting.
