# TIC 320504102: modest positive Sector 75 evidence, no promotion

Sector 75 is the closest available reserved epoch to training Sectors 83–86.
It was selected after inconclusive earlier tests. The unchanged native discovery
ephemeris was recorded before the first download; no period/phase search or
refitting was performed.

The fixed depth is 2330 ± 573 ppm, formal SNR
4.06, with 1 supported events.
This positive evidence is retained. However, it does not meet the standard
follow-up requirement of total SNR ≥5 and at least two supported events.
Timing spread is 0.517 days, versus half-duration
0.0528 days, so the precise-timing gate also fails.

Post-hoc dependent phase controls (the standard follow-up grid) reach maximum
SNR 2.55 over 48 usable shifts.
These are not independent trials or a calibrated survey false-alarm probability.
They neither validate nor exclude the astrophysical interpretation.

Keep the original flags and failed tests. No host attribution, novelty, or likely
planet status is established. The result is supportive only at modest significance
and does not turn the original screening lead into a secure candidate.
