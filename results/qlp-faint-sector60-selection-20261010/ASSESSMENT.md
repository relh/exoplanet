# Sector 60: frozen metadata selection

Selected 1,172 unused targets, excluding all 16,730 prior selections and
cached QLP/SPOC photometry. The existing radius/noise ordering and dated
catalog exclusions are retained. Every target has at least two reserved
product epochs eight or more sector numbers away. Sector 60 was chosen from
the archived remaining-coverage audit for its largest viable sample.

Raw-query, pool and prior-selection hashes are verified in audit.json.
Availability and being unused here do not establish novelty or underexploration.
The first 100 targets form a pilot with unchanged deterministic controls.
