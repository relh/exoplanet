# Sector 58 frozen selection

Selected 448 unused stars from the hash-verified remaining metadata inventory, preserving the existing radius/noise ranking. All prior selections through the full Sector 27 sample are excluded. The first 100 form a pilot, with controls on the first three metadata-ranked stars. Discovery and reserved epochs were fixed before opening light curves. New to this project does not imply scientifically unexplored or novel.

Exclusions: 23,556 prior selections, 14,745 stars without Sector 58 products, and 73 without two sufficiently separated reserved sectors. The pool and all 156 metadata chunks were hash-verified; no selected target had cached QLP/SPOC photometry at selection.
