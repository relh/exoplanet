# HD 32564: retrospective component coherence

Each of the four reported components improves predictions in each omitted calendar
year2010–2013, in both SERVAL and DRS RV reductions. Periods, phases, amplitudes,
instrument offset and trend are fitted to the remaining years. Only pre-upgrade
observations are used; an omitted year must have at least20 usable nights.

Values are held-year chi-square improvement against a training-refitted model
without the listed component, under fixed2m/s nightly white jitter. They are not
false-alarm significances. The weaker49-day component has consistently smaller
improvements. Both reductions share the same spectra. Period neighborhoods came
from an externally reported, already-opened dataset: this is a retrospective
coherence check, not a blind discovery. Activity GP modeling and comprehensive
alias/noise tests are still outstanding; no confirmation or novelty is claimed.
