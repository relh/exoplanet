# TIC 25124525: unresolved signal, no secure source attribution

The discovery plot was inspected. Native QLP refinement gives P=1.3725184828 days,
epoch BTJD 3665.9950711441, duration 0.133056 days and BLS depth 3,887±398 ppm.
The local-depth measurement is 3,968±424 ppm with ten individually supported
events. The dip is weak against substantial scatter and is not a validated planet.

Frozen predictions in sectors 6 and 33 measure −779±437 and 492±398 ppm. Timing
spreads of 6.925 and 4.641 days make these tests inconclusive; neither confirms
recurrence. The exploratory off-phase scan has a selected SNR 2.36. TESS-SPOC87
returned HTTP 404. Ten-minute QLP gives 4,064±463 ppm using the same photons.

The original 11-pixel and wider 21-pixel difference images were inspected. Twenty
generic event windows produce a broad positive background with several localized
positive regions. The maximum formal SNR is 178 near TIC 25124553/709364999,
about 2.55 pixels from the target. This formal value and descriptive moment are
not calibrated source significance or a PRF confidence interval.

A post-hoc spatial-plane check in the 11-pixel image has 19 usable background
pixels at a two-pixel source exclusion radius and too few (four) at three pixels.
The wider image has 173/103 background pixels at those radii. Both fits preserve
multiple source-like residuals; the largest is at [15,10], near bright TIC 25124523,
about 4.038 pixels from the target. All background plots were inspected. Common
image systematics and neighboring variability remain possible; these same-photon
post-hoc checks do not identify a unique eclipsing source or securely locate the
QLP dip on the target.

Five selected TIC entries produced seven DR2-to-DR3 mappings and five distinct
DR3 sources, with no returned classifier or eclipsing-binary rows. Nonmatches
are not evidence of novelty or absence of variability. TIC 25124553 has no QLP
product for sectors 87 or 6. Bright TIC 25124523 measures −459±60 ppm in Sector87
at the fixed target ephemeris, opposite to a transit, and 95±62 ppm in Sector6;
half-phase checks are weak. Thus its positive raw-image residual does not establish
it as the eclipsing contaminant. No period was retuned to those light curves.

Disposition: unresolved, with background/systematic contamination concerns and
no independent recurrence or secure host attribution. Not promoted to a credible
new planet candidate. All available other target epochs were already used by the
frozen follow-up; no additional unreserved target sector is available. Preserve
original and wider images, failed reduction request, catalog mappings/nonmatches,
neighbor checks and inconclusive predictions.
