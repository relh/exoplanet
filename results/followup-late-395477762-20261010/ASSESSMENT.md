# TIC395477762: strong eclipsing-binary evidence

**Not promoted as a planet candidate.** The Sectors80/81 light curve contains
primary and secondary eclipses at P~6.371d. The automated half-phase secondary
check missed the displaced secondary; manual inspection and a recorded
exploratory phase scan identify it near phase0.472.

- Native primary refinement: P6.3714903d, duration0.1584d, depth3,419±32ppm.
- Secondary search: phase0.472, duration0.18d, depth2,117±36ppm, four supported
  eclipses. This is a selected phase/duration scan, not a calibrated significance,
  but the deep repeated secondary strongly favors an eclipsing stellar system.
- QLP training reductions independently reproduce primary dips:3,986±66ppm in
  Sector80 and4,630±61ppm in Sector81. They reduce the same images.
- Previously unopened Sector54 has3,304±44ppm at the frozen primary ephemeris.
  The preset test remains timing-inconclusive because its profile spread0.176d
  exceeds half the event duration; retain that original classification.

See `discovery.png`, `secondary-scan.json`, `frozen.json`, `heldout.json` and
`qlp-training/measurements.json`. A diluted neighboring binary remains possible;
these measurements do not identify which star hosts the eclipses. The refreshed
binary catalogs have no listed binary within126arcsec, illustrating why a catalog
non-match is not proof of planetary nature or novelty.
