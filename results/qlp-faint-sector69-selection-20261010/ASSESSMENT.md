# Sector 69: frozen metadata selection

Selected 512 stars using the existing radius/noise ranking, excluding 22,588 previously selected targets, 15,699 without Sector 69 products, and 23 without two sufficiently separated reserved sectors. All 156 archived coverage chunks and the pool hash were verified. No selected star had cached QLP/SPOC photometry at selection. Independent epochs are reserved before opening discovery light curves.

The first 100 targets form a pilot; expansion requires pilot and deterministic sensitivity review. Selection is new to this project, not evidence that the stars are scientifically unexplored or host novel planets. Archive and catalog snapshots limit the scope.
