# Sector 6 one-reserved exploratory search

All 116 newly selected targets produced usable searches: 104 below threshold,
12 flagged, zero data failures. Neither follow-up policy admitted a peak.
Terminal reconciliation verifies source hashes and empty follow-up inventories.
Reserved observations remain unopened. This batch is distinct from the earlier
exhausted two-reserved-sector selection.

Controls on the first three frozen stars (144776281, 53242247, 200600844)
used 24 trials with exact baseline-array agreement at both detrending windows.
Short trials: seven period/phase recoveries and five screening passes, all twelve
trials within their actual grids. Long trials: six period/phase recoveries and
five passes, but only six trials are in range. All six 8.37-day injections are
outside the actual search grids and cannot be counted as sensitivity failures.

All ten screening passes have valid phase recovery; none is an Earth-radius
trial. In-range longer passes are 5.73 d at three Earth radii on 144776281 and
two/three Earth radii on the other two stars. This small selected sample
(ten passes out of eighteen in-range trials) is not a completeness estimate.
No qualifying signal does not imply no planets.

Metadata inventory excluding the entire batch leaves 1221 unused stars in the
scoped one-distant-reserved-sector pool. Sector 67 is selected next with 110
untouched targets; no corresponding photometry was opened.
