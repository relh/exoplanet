# Additional newer-sector search

Four complete 250-target MAST metadata queries cover the fourth 1,000-star
legacy batch: 10,922 observations, including 1,202 observations of 438 stars
in sectors beyond 85. Source requests, timestamps, counts and hashes are retained.

Before opening their newer photometry, 60 quiet targets were selected using the
same policy as the preceding sample: among at most 200 lowest-noise eligible
stars, retain legacy maximum BLS S/N <8 and take the first 60. Each uses its latest
available adjacent QLP pair beyond Sector 85. Selected pairs span Sectors 87–104.
These newer observations are discovery data, not independent holdouts.

All 60 completed with no errors, no pass of all non-crowding checks, and no
exposure-model companion priority. Forty-five used published uncertainties in
every sector; 15 needed the explicitly enabled empirical fallback in at least
one sector. The fallback never replaces usable archive errors and is recorded
per sector. It is not a calibrated correlated-noise model.

This raises the completed newer-sector QLP sample from 113 to **173 distinct
legacy targets**: 125 with published errors throughout and 48 with a fallback
in at least one sector. The four additional QLP recovery searches for failed
SPOC inputs are separate and use original discovery sectors, not newer pairs.
Overlapping QLP searches are not additional unique stars in the ranked campaign.

No likely new planet is established by this sample. The observed null result
does not establish completeness or absence of planets.
