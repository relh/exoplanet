# Two-sector pilot: 16 searches complete

All 16 frozen metadata-ranked targets produced usable searches: 13 below threshold and three flagged, with no peaks eligible under either original or coverage-diagnostic follow-up policy. Both monitors terminated without opening reserved sectors. The checkpoint verifies raw hashes, frozen target identity, core source hashes and reserved-cache absence; terminal reconciliation matches all 16 identities. No candidate emerged.

The first three metadata targets (143093007, 22339835, 48013569) were all usable control stars, without replacements. Twenty-four exposure-integrated injections were made before binning and detrending. Baseline-loader arrays matched exactly for both detrending windows, and all injected periods were within the actual search grids. Four of 12 short-period trials and seven of 12 longer-period trials passed both period/phase recovery and the noncrowding screening gates: 11 of 24 total. All passing injections were two or three Earth radii; none Earth-sized. Long-period recovery found ten period matches but nine phase-valid matches; these should not be conflated with seven complete passes.

These controls cover only three stars and one phase/duration per family, retaining actual variability. They do not measure population completeness or a calibrated false-positive rate. Two-sector coverage limits event counts. The 4,144 remaining stars in the frozen 4,160-target tier are still unsearched; the successful execution and audited controls support a subsequent expansion using the same gates and validation-sector reservation. This is a new coverage tier within the dated nearby faint-midradius selection, not a claim to have searched all TESS data or proved novelty.
