# Sector 27 metadata selection

The next sector is selected by viable target count in the hash-verified remaining inventory. All prior selections through the full Sector 69 sample are excluded, including its still-running expansion. Discovery and reserved epochs are fixed before any photometry. The first 100 targets form a pilot, with deterministic controls on the first three metadata-ranked stars. Selection is new to this project, not proof of scientific novelty or absence of earlier research.

Selected 456 stars; exclusions are 23,100 prior selections, 15,149 without Sector 27 products and 117 without two sufficiently separated reserved epochs. Pool and all 156 coverage chunks were hash-verified. No selected target had cached QLP/SPOC photometry at selection.
