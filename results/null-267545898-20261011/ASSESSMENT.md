# TIC 267545898: zero exceedances in conditional shuffle controls

None of 48 block-shuffled discovery curves matched or exceeded the original maximum box-search power: 0/16 at each of 0.5, 1 and 2-day blocks. This supports a coherent discovery-period signal within the chosen control family. It does not distinguish a transit from stellar variability and does not remove the broad/asymmetric morphology concern.

The full period/duration maximum is searched in each control, but downstream screening gates are not reapplied. Finite blocks disrupt long correlations, introduce seams and may retain fragments of a real signal. Zero exceedances in a small conditional sample is not zero false-alarm risk, a calibrated survey-wide probability, or planet validation.

Verification checks 48 permutation bijections, raw/code hashes and outcome counts, reruns the observed search and one control at each length. The remaining 45 outcomes are reconciled without rerunning. Only original sectors 27–28 are used. Reserved sector 68 remains unopened at this stage.
