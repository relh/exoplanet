# Separate recovery stream for the three-sector campaign

The same previously exercised time-only policy handles baseline-limit failures in the three-sector campaign. It tries the latest three then two originally assigned sectors and freezes the first usable window fitting the unchanged 120-day guard before searching its period. Reserved sectors remain untouched during selection. Nonbaseline errors are retained as explicitly deferred cases for individual review.

The original failed attempts remain failures. Alternate windows add no stars, and no periodogram or diagnostic plot establishes a planet. The stream is live; its plan is not evidence that any recovery has completed.
