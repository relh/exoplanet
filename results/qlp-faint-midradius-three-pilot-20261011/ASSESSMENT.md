# Three-sector pilot: 16 usable searches, no qualifying signal

The first 16 stars in the frozen three-sector metadata ranking were searched with the unchanged pipeline. All are usable: nine stay below threshold and seven have peaks failing screening checks. Neither follow-up policy admits a peak; all reserved QLP sectors remain unopened. No new credible candidate follows.

Selection identity, raw hashes, terminal results and the two zero-follow-up inventories are reconciled. The initial follow-up watchers failed before their parent plan existed; those terminal startup errors are retained, and both watchers then completed successfully from the unchanged scripts. No photometry was opened in the failed starts.

Controls use the first three metadata-ranked stars, TIC 444001911, 232020909 and 159629840. All three have usable original searches, so none is excluded or replaced. All 24 injection trials are terminal and inside the actual search grid. Baseline arrays match the discovery loader exactly at both detrending windows. Short-period trials give five period/phase recoveries and two screening passes, both at two Earth radii and 0.83 days. Longer-period trials give ten period/phase recoveries and five screening passes at two or three Earth radii. All seven passes meet the timing checks; none is Earth-sized. No trial passes on TIC 232020909. These three stars and single phase/duration per family do not establish survey completeness.

This pilot covers 16 of the 1,509 newly selected three-sector stars, disjoint from the four-sector tier. The remaining 1,493 are outside the pilot and now form a separate running campaign. It does not exhaust TESS or establish novelty.
