# Sector 70 exploratory search

All 25 frozen targets produced usable searches below the diagnostic
screening threshold. There were no flagged targets or data failures,
and neither follow-up policy found an eligible peak. Reserved sectors
remain unopened. No new planet candidate emerged.

Controls used the first three frozen stars, TIC 37745741, 47095952 and
293434440, without selecting by measured quietness. All baseline arrays
agree exactly at both detrending windows for both control runs. All 24
injections are within the actual searched period grids.

Short-period controls yielded ten period matches, nine phase matches and
five screening passes: all three stars at 0.83 days and two Earth radii,
plus the first two stars at 2.37 days and two Earth radii. Long-period
controls yielded twelve period matches, eleven phase matches and one
screening pass: TIC 293434440 at 5.73 days and three Earth radii. All six
passes satisfy the phase check. No Earth-radius trial passed.

One period match in each control family failed the phase check and is
not a timing recovery. Many longer-period trials fail event-count,
odd/even-measurability or alternative-detrending checks despite recovering
the period and phase. Original gates remain unchanged. Three stars with
one phase and duration per control family are not a completeness or
false-alarm calibration; the null search does not exclude planets.

The metadata inventory leaves 246 untouched stars in the scoped archive
snapshot. Sector 59 has 20 targets selected next, with complete historical
exclusions, 156 coverage-chunk hashes checked, exact expected set and zero
prior overlap. No Sector 59 photometry has been opened for that selection.
The survey remains limited to the existing target pool and archive snapshot;
new to this search is not proof of novelty or underexploration.
