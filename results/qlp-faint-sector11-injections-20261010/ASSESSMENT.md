# Sector 11 sensitivity controls

Ten of 12 periods were recovered, eight also passed the phase check, and six passed all non-crowding screening gates. All six two-Earth-radius trials passed; none of the six Earth-radius trials passed.

All 12 injected periods lie inside the reconstructed grid. Baseline arrays match discovery exactly under both detrending windows on all three targets. These sparse, single-phase and single-duration controls show limited sensitivity and do not establish completeness. Crowding and host attribution are not tested by flux injections.
