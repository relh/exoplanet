# Sector 84 exploratory search

All 179 targets produced usable searches: 136 below threshold and 43 flagged,
zero data failures. One original-policy peak (TIC 432280723) and no two-event
diagnostic peaks reached follow-up. Reserved Sector 57 timing is inconclusive.
Wide raw pixels and deep primary/secondary eclipses in nearby apertures favor
neighboring-source contamination; the individual
assessment records the full source investigation. No securely attributed
new planet candidate is claimed.

The first three frozen stars (432277901, 53828786, 423975007) received 24
controls, all within actual search grids and with identical baseline arrays
under both detrending windows. Short trials: nine period recoveries, eight
phase recoveries and five screening passes. Long trials: twelve period/phase
recoveries and four passes. All nine passes have valid phase recovery; none is
an Earth-radius trial. Long passes are 5.73 d, two/three Earth radii on the
second and third stars. Period recovery alone is not enough.
This small selected control sample does not establish completeness or absence
of planets among rejected signals.

Metadata inventory excluding the whole selection leaves 1337 unused stars.
Sector 6 is selected next with 116 untouched targets in this one-reserved tier
(distinct from the exhausted two-reserved Sector 6 selection). No photometry
has been opened for these next targets.
