# TIC 233194992: diagnostic lead, not promoted

The first peak in QLP Sectors 83–86 fails alternative-detrending consistency
and has unresolved aperture crowding. Native refinement gives period
13.2869361471 days, epoch BTJD 3571.6887898886 and duration 0.072 days.
The fixed training depth is 7,483 ± 834 ppm (formal SNR 8.97), with four
supported events. The inspected plot shows structured scatter after gaps and
an irregular folded dip. The selected SNR is not a discovery significance.

Reserved Sectors 14 and 15 were uncached when the ephemeris was frozen.
Sector 14 measures 78 ± 1,410 ppm with only four in-event points; Sector 15
has no usable fixed-event depth. Prediction spreads 0.6287 and 0.6204 days
exceed half the duration, so both tests are timing-inconclusive. Missing coverage
is not a planet nondetection. The selected off-phase dip SNR is 3.41, compared
with a brightening SNR 3.73; these exploratory maxima are uncalibrated.

[Same-epoch reduction attempt](../../qlp-faint-233194992-reduction-20261010/results.json)
uses the frozen ephemeris. QLP depths in Sectors 83–86 are 3,706 ± 2,383,
8,455 ± 3,356, 7,005 ± 1,213 and 8,905 ± 1,541 ppm, respectively.
All four SPOC downloads return HTTP 404. This provides no separate-reduction
confirmation or negative evidence; the download failures remain in the results.

The fitted period and epoch are close to the weakened TIC 198186473 signal
(period 13.2809582634 days, epoch 3571.6174803282) from the same sectors.
This is a reason to investigate a shared artifact, not proof of one. No
host attribution, independent recovery or novelty claim is supported.
The lead remains diagnostic-only and unpromoted.

A [fixed cross-ephemeris comparison](../../qlp-faint-13day-pair-20261010/ASSESSMENT.md)
finds non-overlapping predicted windows and no dip in either curve at the other
star's ephemeris. The stars are 8.05 degrees apart. A shared identical event is
not established; a broader systematic remains unproven.
