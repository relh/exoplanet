# Remaining metadata coverage after Sector 52

The immutable archived coverage queries contain 12,620 unused viable targets
after excluding 15,429 distinct prior selections and cached photometry. Sector
76 has the largest overlapping sample (1,301), followed by 75 (1,246),
15 (1,241), 16 (1,229) and 60 (1,192). Sector counts overlap and must not be summed.

Every viable training sector has at least two other product epochs eight or
more sector numbers away. This metadata-only audit opens no light curves.
Raw-query hashes, the parent pool and every prior selection are checked and
recorded in coverage.json. Archive availability and dated catalog exclusions
limit the sample. Unused here does not establish underexploration or novelty.
