# Sector52 frozen selection

1,507unused stars selected by metadata in existing radius/noise order.
13,922previous selections are excluded;23,357pool stars lack Sector52 and36
lack two sufficiently separated reserved sectors. Each selected target has
unique archive products and no preexisting QLP/SPOC target photometry cache.
The original dated catalog exclusions are preserved. This does not establish
novelty or absence of earlier astronomical study.

The first100stars form a pilot. Deterministic injection checks use the first
three frozen targets before any expansion decision. All plans, selection
inputs and raw/source hashes are retained; reserved epochs are opened only
through recorded follow-up policies.
