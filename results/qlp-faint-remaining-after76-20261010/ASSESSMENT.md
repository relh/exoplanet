# Remaining coverage after reserving the full Sector 76 selection

The metadata audit identifies 11,319 unused viable stars, excluding 16,730
previously selected targets, including all 1,301 targets in the ongoing
Sector 76 campaign. Sector 60 has the largest overlapping remaining sample
(1,172), followed by Sector 20 (1,158), 47 (1,111) and 74 (1,105).
Counts overlap and must not be summed. No photometry was opened by this audit.

Hashes verify the complete archived query chunks, parent pool and prior
selections. Each viable training epoch has at least two product epochs eight
or more sector numbers away; cached photometry is excluded. Archived coverage
and dated catalog exclusions limit this inventory. It does not prove novelty,
underexploration, current archive completeness or transit detectability.
