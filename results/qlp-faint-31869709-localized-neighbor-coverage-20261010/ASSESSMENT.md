# Archive coverage for the localized contaminant candidates

After two difference images placed the short-period signal off TIC 31869709,
a complete exact-target-name MAST query searched TIC 31869705 and TIC 31869706
in QLP, TESS-SPOC and SPOC. It returned zero observations. The original request,
response metadata and raw hash are retained in this directory.

This is distinct from the earlier query for the nearest positional neighbors,
TIC 650349469 and TIC 31869713. The sources tested here were selected from the
Sector 29/30 difference-image moments. No direct neighbor light curve could be
measured. Missing products do not exclude eclipses or establish a depth limit;
alias and coordinate-only observations were not searched by this query.

The two-sector off-target evidence remains strong, but the exact source among
TIC 31869705/31869706 is unresolved. Neither a planet nor a binary classification
is established. Higher-resolution photometry or a calibrated spatial analysis
would be needed to distinguish these close sources. No external contact or
candidate submission was made.
