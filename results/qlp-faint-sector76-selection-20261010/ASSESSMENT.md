# Sector 76: frozen metadata selection

Selected 1,301 unused stars in the existing radius/noise order, excluding all
15,429 previous selections. Each target has at least two reserved epochs eight
or more sector numbers away. No prior QLP/SPOC target photometry was cached.
Archive-query, pool and selection hashes are recorded in audit.json.

Sector 76 was chosen from the remaining-coverage audit because it offers the
largest remaining viable sample. This establishes availability, not novelty or
underexploration. The first 100 targets form a pilot; unchanged short- and
long-period injection controls are required before expanding to the rest.
