# TIC469309982: later-sector telemetry and event sensitivity

At the previously selected clock,18 event windows in opened sectors63/64 were examined in background, two pointing-correction and two centroid columns. Ninety local linear-trend residual measurements independently reproduce with polynomial fits; raw inputs are hashed. The plot was inspected.

Cycle515 coincides with a pointing discontinuity/gap; cycle522 has a substantial background trend change. Their residual/scatter ratios reach about3.9 and−4.0 respectively. These ratios are descriptive, not standard-error significance or causal attribution.

Posthoc whole-cycle omission, keeping the clock and existing flattening fixed, shows different behavior in SAP and PDCSAP. Omitting522 reduces SAP formal SNR from3.45/4.47 to1.14/1.95 for the two windows. Omitting515 instead increases it to5.48/6.68. Omitting both gives3.13/4.10. Corrected PDCSAP remains at5.13/5.57 without522 and5.71/6.05 without both, close to its full-data5.72/6.16. Full results and input hashes are in omission.json and its reproducing script.

Thus the uncorrected flux evidence is sensitive to spacecraft-related structure, while the corrected dip is not solely contributed by these two cycles. This neither confirms a planet nor proves an artifact. Selection used sector64 and telemetry has now been inspected; no independent significance is claimed. Source ambiguity, timing uncertainty and possible secondary remain unresolved.
