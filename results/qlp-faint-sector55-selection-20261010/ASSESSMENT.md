# Sector 55 frozen selection

Selected 395 unused stars, preserving the existing radius/noise ranking. Excluded 24,004 prior selections, 13,876 targets without Sector 55 products, and 547 without two sufficiently separated reserved sectors. The pool and all 156 archived coverage chunks were hash-verified; no selected star had cached QLP/SPOC photometry.

The first 100 form a pilot with deterministic controls on the first three metadata-ranked stars. Discovery and reserved epochs are fixed before opening photometry. New to this project is not evidence of scientific novelty or underexploration. Dated catalog/archive and stellar-selection limits remain.
