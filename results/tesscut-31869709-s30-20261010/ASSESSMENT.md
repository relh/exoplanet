# TIC 31869709: second-sector off-target evidence

The unchanged short-period prediction produces 70 usable Sector 30 events.
The selected difference peak has formal pixel SNR 36.95; the moment lies
2.7576 pixels from the target at RA 53.02543960, Dec −68.81181651 degrees.
Visual inspection shows a compact positive concentration separated from the
target and away from the cutout boundary, on a different camera/CCD orientation
than Sector 29. TIC 31869705 is 0.023 pixels from this moment, with TIC 31869706
0.329 pixels away. The two neighbors remain close relative to TESS resolution.

Sector 29 also places the signal off target (2.1208 pixels), but the two sky
moments differ by 10.45 arcseconds. These uncalibrated moments are therefore
insufficient for a unique close-neighbor assignment. Both images strongly favor
contamination of TIC 31869709; TIC 31869705 is a plausible source, not a confirmed
host. Neither image determines whether the source is an eclipsing binary or planet.

The images use training-epoch FFI photons and are post-hoc localization diagnostics.
Formal selected-pixel errors do not incorporate background or PRF uncertainty;
catalog positions are not proper-motion propagated. Original reserved timing
failures, detrending flags and missing SPOC data remain in the record.
