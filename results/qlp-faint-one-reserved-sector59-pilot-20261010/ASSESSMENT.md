broad and smoothly varying, with fitted duration 0.18 days at period

yielded seven period/phase matches and four screening passes: the first two

ten period/phase matches and one screening pass: TIC 82491393 at 5.73 days
and three Earth radii. All five passes satisfy the phase check; no Earth-radius
trial passed. These selected controls do not establish survey completeness
or a false-alarm rate, and the null search does not exclude planets.
