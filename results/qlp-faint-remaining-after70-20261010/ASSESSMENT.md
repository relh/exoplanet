# Remaining viable metadata after the Sector 70 selection

There are 15,932 unused distinct stars with at least one archived sector and
two sufficiently separated reserved epochs under the existing selection
policy. This excludes the full 1,931-star Sector 70 selection, including
its still-running expansion, and all earlier selections. No photometry is
opened by this audit; cached target photometry is excluded.

The largest remaining sector sample is Sector 72 (1,805 stars), followed by
Sector 60 (1,508), Sector 52 (1,507), Sector 19 (1,484), Sector 20 (1,482),
and Sector 73 (1,480). These overlapping counts are not summed and do not
establish novelty or historical underexploration. The archival metadata
snapshot and original dated catalog exclusions remain limitations. A next
selection must recheck prior selections and caches before opening data.

Full source hashes, exclusions and star/sector inventories are in coverage.json.
