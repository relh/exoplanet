# Training-only review of strong early pilot peaks

None of these three peaks is promoted as a planet candidate. This is a post-hoc
visual review of the first three strong flagged targets, not an independent test
or an exhaustive audit of the unfinished100-star pilot. No reserved data opened.

- TIC38698751: P0.602098d, formal SNR24.26, conditional radius0.84Earth.
  The attractive nominal radius is insufficient: the fit spans a large fraction
  of the period, event depths are inconsistent, and the time series has large
  excursions around observing interruptions. The broad folded modulation does
  not establish a transit.
- TIC424389265: P0.908110d, formal SNR49.02. The folded series visibly has broad
  sinusoidal modulation, with a second minimum. Sinusoidal/inverted-signal,
  secondary and duty-cycle flags are retained. This peak is not a transit candidate.
- TIC310128088: P14.647603d, formal SNR24.09, depth4.61%, conditional radius3.90Earth.
  Only two events are supported, with2 and5in-window points. Both coincide with
  large excursions near the start of observing segments in Sector11; earlier
  training sectors do not provide repeated comparable events. The apparent
  period is therefore not established. The figure shows structured excursions
  extending well outside the fitted short event; do not promote or treat the
  conditional radius as a measured planet radius.

Figures use native QLP sampling and the unchanged training ephemeris. All original
flags and source hashes are in review-inputs.json and the linked discovery files.
The interpretation of gap-associated artifacts is provisional; no physical
classification of these stars or exclusion of other planets is claimed.
