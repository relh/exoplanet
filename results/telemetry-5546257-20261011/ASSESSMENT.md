Source coincidence strengthens contamination concerns but does not explain the period or establish a complete physical model. The target remains a distinct catalog object.

Whole-cycle removal from saved SAP/PDCSAP light curves leaves all four fixed-clock measurements unchanged. The relevant in-transit data have already been removed by common flux validity and light-curve cleaning; those image events therefore do not drive the measured dip. This exposes a mismatch between usable image events and cleaned photometric events, not disappearance of the astrophysical-looking signal. The full image stacks and all unfavorable fits are retained.

TIC5546257 stays unresolved with stronger neighboring-source evidence. It is not promoted to a new on-target planet. The good sector35 fit after posthoc omission is conditional; sector36 residuals and catalog-period mismatch still require investigation. All omissions were chosen after inspecting telemetry, and no independent source probability is claimed.
