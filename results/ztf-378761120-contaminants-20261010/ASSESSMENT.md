# Ground photometry identifies the contaminating eclipse source

At the unchanged TESS period/epoch, TIC378761130 shows~0.24mag eclipses in both
ZTF g and r and in two fields. The primary g/r sources coincide with the TIC
position to0.03arcsec. This supports the off-target TESS image and aperture
findings; the shallow signal is not a planet around TIC378761120.

See `analysis.json`, `fixed-phase.png`, `dilution.json` and raw-download provenance.
All cone objects are retained. We did not optimize period or phase on ZTF data,
combine unrelated OIDs, or treat insufficiently sampled objects as non-detections.
Magnitude errors are formal and do not include red-noise/source-selection trials.
The depth alone does not establish the companion's mass or definitively classify
the eclipsing system. The source-attribution conclusion is the relevant result.
