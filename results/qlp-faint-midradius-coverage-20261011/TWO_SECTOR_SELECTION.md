# Additional two-sector coverage

The frozen metadata audit excludes all 1,073 four-sector and 1,509 three-sector targets, including their unprocessed members. Of the 14,742 remaining stars, 14,342 have QLP metadata. Exactly 4,160 satisfy the latest-two-consecutive-sectors rule with at least two validation sectors each at least eight sector numbers away; 10,582 do not. The selected stars span 90 training-sector combinations. Query hashes and complete metadata paging were checked, and all selected photometry caches were absent at selection time.

The population retains the existing nearby, faint midradius limits: distance at most 120 pc, T magnitude above 12 and at most 13.5, stellar radius above 0.5 and at most 0.8 solar radii, effective temperature at most 5,200 K, and catalog crowding at most 0.2. Ordering uses metadata, not measured quietness. These are additional search targets, not candidates or evidence of novelty. Two-sector coverage limits long-period event counts; the original screening gates remain unchanged.

The first 16 metadata-ranked stars form a bounded pilot. The pilot and its controls are complete. The remaining 4,144-target campaign has now launched; it remains in progress, with separate expansion controls and immutable checkpoints.
