# Additional three-sector target selection

The complete, hashed coverage metadata for the original 17,324-star faint midradius pool was reused without new photometry. Excluding all 1,073 stars in the frozen four-sector tier leaves 16,251 stars, of which 15,851 have QLP metadata. Of these remaining stars, 1,509 have a latest consecutive three-sector discovery window and at least two reserved sectors each at least eight sector numbers from every discovery sector. The other 14,742 do not meet this rule.

The selected stars span 74 training-sector combinations. Their original metadata ranking is retained, and cache absence was checked for every selected star before freezing. The new selection is disjoint from the full four-sector tier, including its not-yet-processed targets. Its dated catalog exclusions are inherited from the original pool; they do not prove novelty or underexploration by others.

This is a list of search targets, not planet candidates or a completeness result. The unchanged discovery gates and 120-day baseline guard apply when searched. The first 16 form a separate pilot; the other 1,493 remain outside that pilot.
