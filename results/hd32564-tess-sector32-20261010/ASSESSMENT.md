# HD 32564: additional Sector 32 transit screen

Both QLP and TESS-SPOC screens finished below threshold with no data errors.
QLP's strongest selected peak has period 0.60838 days and formal SNR 5.14;
SPOC's has period 9.20732 days and formal SNR 6.44. Neither qualifies for
verification, and these periodogram peaks are not planet candidates.

This post-hoc extension follows the negative Sector 5 search of the external
RV lead HD32564 = TIC213078996. Each reduction uses Sector32 only; the reductions
share the same images and are not independent observations. Sector98 remains
unopened by this run. The short baseline cannot test every reported RV period,
and the blind transit search neither rejects nontransiting planets nor supplies
an injection-calibrated transit depth exclusion. The original RV evidence and
its activity/alias limitations remain unchanged. No discovery claim is made.
