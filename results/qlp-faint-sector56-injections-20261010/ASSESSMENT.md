# Sector 56 sensitivity controls

Of 12 injections, eight matched a top-three period, seven also matched phase and passed all non-crowding gates. All six two-Earth-radius injections passed; only one of six Earth-radius injections passed. The extra period match failed phase and screening.

All trials were within the reconstructed search grid; bounds were verified against the immutable audit. Uninjected arrays match discovery exactly for both detrending windows on all three stars. These are sparse controls on three preselected stars, one phase and duration per trial, not a completeness estimate. Published radius and undiluted depth assumptions apply; source attribution and crowding remain untested by flux injections.
