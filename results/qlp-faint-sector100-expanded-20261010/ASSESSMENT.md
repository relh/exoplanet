# Sector 100 faint-star expansion: complete

All 1,499 frozen targets finished: 1,408 below screen threshold, 91 flagged,
zero data errors. Combined with the completed 100-star pilot, this campaign
searched 1,599 selected stars. The original-policy watcher completed three
follow-ups; the coverage-diagnostic watcher had none. Both exited successfully.

TIC 164293311 and TIC 459258444 resolve to known neighboring Gaia eclipsing
binaries. TIC 383941028 remains an off-target signal with uncertain exact source.
All three have individual assessments linked at the top of README. No likely
new planet is established. Broad timing uncertainty makes all reserved-sector
prediction checks inconclusive, even the positive Sector 36 measurement for
459258444. No negative fixed-phase measurement is treated as an exclusion.

The metadata-based selection, immutable target records, raw-data hashes,
injection controls, failed checks, and terminal watcher inventories are retained.
Controls recovered all 12 injected periods and checked phases; nine passed
non-crowding gates. This limited sample is not a survey completeness estimate.
The actual cleaned baseline restricts each period search. Single-sector fits
may poorly predict years-old events. Catalog nonmatches never establish novelty.

The reconciliation script verifies target inventories, raw files, source hashes,
and all three follow-up artifact sets. Its report is reconciliation.json.
