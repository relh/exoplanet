# Completed 100-star faint QLP pilot

All 100 frozen targets processed successfully: 46 below the diagnostic threshold,
54 flagged, zero errors. No peak passed the original non-crowding screen or the
separately labeled detrending-only diagnostic category. The follow-up watcher
finished with no records and opened no reserved sectors. This is a negative
screening result, not evidence that these stars have no planets.

The search retained the top three periodogram peaks for every target. Strong
flagged examples were inspected at native cadence: TIC38698751 and TIC424389265
show broad variability; TIC310128088 is driven by sparse, gap-associated events;
TIC329291443 has inconsistent depths and structured excursions. Assessments and
plots are retained in the adjacent shape-review directories. Automatic flags on
other targets remain screening evidence, not definitive stellar classifications.

All source hashes match the frozen plan. The final audit inventories all target
files; quality.json records noise and flag distributions. No scientific search
threshold changed during this pilot. The known-planet controls show limited
sensitivity, while 12 deterministic raw-flux injections recover all injected
periods/phases and ten pass signal-quality checks. Neither experiment measures
survey completeness; injections on three small stars do not establish equivalent
Earth-radius sensitivity for the larger stars in the remaining selection.

Decision: proceed with the remaining 1,845 frozen targets, retaining four-sector
training, untouched reserved epochs, original thresholds, all top-three peaks,
and separately labeled detrending diagnostics. The complete 1,945-star selection
covers only the stated magnitude/radius/distance/coverage cuts; catalog exclusions
and metadata availability do not prove that these stars are unexplored or novel.
