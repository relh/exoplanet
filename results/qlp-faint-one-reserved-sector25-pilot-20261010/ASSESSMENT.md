flagged, zero failures. Neither follow-up policy yielded an eligible peak;
reserved sectors remain unopened. TIC 286424114 has peaks near 0.50–0.54 days
with comparable inverted signals, large duty cycles and unmeasurable

and fail alternative detrending. These flags remain binding. No securely
attributed new planet candidate emerged.

Controls used the first three frozen stars, TIC 258238684, 21744632 and
332391129, without selecting for measured quietness. All three baseline
arrays agree exactly at both detrending windows in both control runs.
Short-period trials yielded four period matches, three phase matches and
one screening pass: TIC 21744632 at 0.83 days and two Earth radii, with valid
phase. No Earth-radius trial passed. One period match failed the phase
check; two other short-period signals remained below required thresholds.

The 12 long-period trials yielded no period matches or passes. Six trials
at 5.73 days were inside the searched grid; six at 8.37 days were outside it
and cannot count as in-range sensitivity failures. Clean baselines of
16.33–16.46 days limit the actual grid to about 6.53–6.59 days. Combined,
one of 18 in-grid injections passed screening and timing. This weak recovery
is a material limit on the null search result. Three stars, one phase and
one duration per control family do not establish survey completeness or
a false-alarm rate.
