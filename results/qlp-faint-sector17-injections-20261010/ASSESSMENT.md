# Sector 17 controls: limited recovery and explicit search bounds

First three frozen stars: TIC 240821194, 16917330 and 268626115. Clean
baselines are 20.271, 20.750 and 20.750 days. Exact baseline arrays match
both production flattening windows before injection; all 12 attempts finished
without errors. Original noise and variability remain in the injected data.

Short controls recover 5/12 periods and checked phases, with 4/12 full
noncrowding passes. No one-Earth-radius control recovers. TIC 16917330's
two-Earth-radius 2.37-day signal also fails period recovery. TIC 268626115's
two-Earth-radius 2.37-day signal has a period/phase match but is below the
screening threshold. All short periods are inside the actual search bounds.
These results show poor sensitivity to the smaller injected signals.

No gates were relaxed. Three stars and one phase/duration per period group
cannot establish survey completeness, a false-positive rate or planet absence.
Reserved epochs were not opened by these controls.
