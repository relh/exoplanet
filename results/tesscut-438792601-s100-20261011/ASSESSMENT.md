# TIC 438792601: repeated off-target dimming

The Sector 100 raw cutout was analyzed using the unchanged four-sector discovery ephemeris and the same neighbor catalog as Sector 99. Thirty-three event windows contribute. The inspected difference image shows a compact positive source about 2.03 pixels from the target, near the faint blended group seen in Sector 99. The two image moments differ by about 3.35 arcseconds. TIC 288891067 is the closest listed source to both moments, but other faint catalog entries are blended at this resolution; these moments do not identify the exact variable star.

This repeats the off-target evidence at another observing epoch and strengthens the contamination interpretation. The epoch was already part of discovery training, and its raw pixels are the same photons used by QLP: this is a source-location diagnostic, not independent transit confirmation. The peak formal pixel SNR of 32.1 and the image moments are not calibrated confidence measures. Background residuals, the pixel response and unpropagated catalog positions limit the inference.

Raw, fit and neighbor hashes are verified in `verification.json`. No on-target planet claim, novelty claim or external submission follows.
