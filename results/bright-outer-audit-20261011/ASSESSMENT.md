# Bright outer-distance sample reconciliation

The metadata pool contains 15 stars at 120–200 pc meeting the frozen brightness, radius, temperature, crowding and dated catalog-exclusion cuts. Thirteen distinct targets were searched in five disjoint batches: nine below threshold and four flagged, with no data failures or qualifying follow-ups. Selection hashes, target identities, terminal inventories, raw hashes and absence of opened reserved light curves were checked.

The remaining two targets, TIC 9271247 (Sector 21) and TIC 335771678 (Sector 91), each have only one QLP sector in the archived metadata. They cannot provide a separate observing epoch under the current validation rule. They are unsearched, not nondetections.

Individual batch controls show limited and variable recovery and no passing Earth-radius injections. No survey completeness or planet exclusion follows. This audit covers this specific 15-star population, not all TESS data or every viable target. Earlier unresolved source assignments remain unresolved.
