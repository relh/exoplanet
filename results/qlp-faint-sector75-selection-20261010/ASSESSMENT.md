# Sector 75: frozen metadata selection

Selected 660 unused stars after excluding all 21,285 prior selections, including the complete Sector 86 sample. Existing radius/noise ordering, dated catalog exclusions and two reserved sectors at least eight sector numbers away are retained. Raw archived coverage and pool hashes verified; no selected target had cached photometry. Availability is not evidence of novelty or underexploration. A 100-star pilot and deterministic sensitivity controls precede expansion.
