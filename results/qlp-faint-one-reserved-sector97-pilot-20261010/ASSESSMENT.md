# Sector 97 exploratory search

69 usable searches: 49 below threshold, 20 flagged, no data failures.
Two diagnostic-only follow-ups: TIC 123404957 (detrending disagreement,
four supported events) and TIC 164698536 (detrending disagreement, two events).
Both reserved Sector 3 predictions are timing-inconclusive. SPOC light curves
and processed pixels are unavailable for both. Raw pixels do not securely
locate a transit: extended column/background structure for the former,
negative target residual after background removal for the latter. Assessments
retain these limitations. No credible new planet candidate.

Controls used first three frozen stars 302498974, 408110150, 408118225.
All 24 trials are in-grid and baseline arrays agree exactly for both detrending
windows. Short: 11 period/phase recoveries, eight screening passes.
Long: 12 period/phase recoveries and 12 passes. All 20 passes have valid
phase. Earth-radius passes are 0.83-day trials on 302498974 and 408118225.
These selected tests are not survey completeness or false-alarm calibration.

Selection provenance audit found a copied sector substitution had altered
historical exclusion paths in the selection script. Original artifacts are
retained. An independent full-inventory verifier proves all 69 selected stars
have zero overlap with complete prior selections and exactly match the
expected metadata-ranked set. Thus the searched set remains valid; original
exclusion counts should not be used as complete provenance.

Metadata leaves 862 unused stars. Sector 72 is next with 67 untouched targets.
Its analogous exclusion-script error is documented; full-inventory verification
and a corrected-exclusions rerun both recover the exact same ordered list.
Future selectors must obtain historical paths from the inventory rather than
replace sector text inside them. No next-sample photometry opened.
