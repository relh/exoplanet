## TIC 235596068: likely eclipsing stellar system

The 4.80206-day primary dip is about 7,924 ppm deep. QLP shows it in discovery
Sectors 52/53 (7,459/7,228 ppm, formal S/N 20.5/22.0). Inspection revealed another
deep dip away from phase 0.5. An exploratory 181-phase, three-duration scan found
phase 0.655, duration 0.04 day, depth 8,669 ppm and formal S/N 34.95, supported by
eight events. The full scan and discovery snapshot are retained. This is not a
blind test or calibrated search significance.

QLP also shows the secondary in both sectors: 7,539/7,041 ppm, formal S/N
19.9/18.2. Sector 52 primary and secondary difference images are both central;
they cannot resolve an overlapping blend. Comparable deep eclipses displaced
from half phase favor an eccentric eclipsing stellar system. Do not promote as
a planet candidate. The original phase-0.5 secondary check missed this feature;
vetting must inspect the entire folded orbit. Reserved 74/79/80 remain unopened.
Host assignment remains unresolved.
