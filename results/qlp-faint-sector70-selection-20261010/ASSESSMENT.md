# Sector 70 metadata selection

Frozen before opening target photometry: 1,931 unused stars, preserving the
existing pool radius/noise ranking and dated catalog exclusions. The metadata
inventory covers 38,822 stars and excludes all 10,186 previous selections,
including the full currently running Sector 97 sample. A further 26,485 lack
Sector 70 and 220 lack two sufficiently separated reserved sectors.

Each selected star has a unique archive product per sector, no preexisting
QLP/SPOC target photometry cache, and at least two reserved sectors separated
from Sector 70 by eight or more sector numbers. This is a reproducible
selection within the archived pool, not a claim that these stars have never
been studied elsewhere. Selection source hashes and the full frozen list are
in audit.json and selected.json.

The first 100 metadata-ranked targets form a pilot. Expansion requires review
of that pilot and deterministic injection sensitivity checks. Reserved epochs
are opened only through the recorded follow-up policies.
